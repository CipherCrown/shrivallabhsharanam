    EKADASHI VRAT

    Utpathit Ekadashi - Vrat Katha

    Shri Vishnu pretending
    After listening to Indra's plea, Bhagwan Shri Vishnu said “Devraj! (knowlingly He pretend as if He knows nothing about this development)
    How is this Dhanav?
    How he looks?
    How strong is he?
    How is the place where this Dusht (evil soul) is living?

    Discribing Moor
    Indra said “Deveshwar! In olden times, in Bhramaji’s Vansh (generation) a Mahan Aasur named Taalaj was born, who was furiously dangerous and his son was more furious and famous by the name Moor. Moor was extremely ardent, a great intrepid and for Devtas he was dreadful. He lived in famous city, named Chandravati. He defeated all Devtas and threw them out of Swarg-lok. He crowned someone else as Swarg’s Indra and gave him Swarg’s throne and also established new Agni, Chandrama, Surya, Vayu and Varun as Swarg’s Devtas. Janardhan! I am telling the truth, every person is new in Swarg-lok. Devtas have lost their identity”.

    War with Moor
    After listening the sad tale of Indra, Bhagwan Jarnadhan became mercifully angry, taking all Devtas along with Him, He went to Chandravati puri. Here Devtas saw the daitiyah was roaring loudly, declaring war with them, and eventually defeating them all, the Devtas in fear ran here and there, seeing all this fear and commotion Bhagwan Vishnu intervened and told Moor to “stop, this carnage”, Listening to Bhagwan Vishnu Moor Dhanav challenged Him. Listening to Moor's challenging word's, Banghwan said “wicked dhanav (demon) see my arms and muscles”, saying so Bhagwan Vishnu started sending His divine arrows at Dhanav to kill him. Dhanav feared and ran. Now Bhagwan Vishnu used His Sudarshan Chakra on Dhaitiyah army and killed hundreds of millions of his strong army men. Moor in his army had many dhanav's who had divine boons accquired by years of penance from various Vedic Gods and Goddesses. Moor virtually had accquired an army of impossible alway victourios soldiers, no one could defeat his army, many Dhanav commando's had accquired boons from Mahadev and ammassed very many powerful and divine weapons, energy., they were all great in the art of Maya Yidh Banghwan Vishnu systematically killed all of them and then Bhagwan Madhu-sudhan went to Bhadrikashram. There was a cave named Singh-vati, which was 12 youjan in length with only one cave entrance. He went in and pretended to be in sleep.

    Meanwhile Dhanav Moor who earlier had ran from the battle ground was trying his best to kill Bhagwan Shri Vishnu with different strategies and therefore he secretly followed Him there. He went inside the cave where Bhagwan Vishnu was resting in absolute calmness; Moor was surprise to see Shri Hari fearlessly clam and resting. Moor thaught it is the best time to kill Shri Hari as he thought this Devta is giving fear to Dhanavs therefore I should definitely kill Him.

    Appearance of divine Girl
    Bhagwan Shri Krishna said “Yudhistar! While Moor thought of killing Bhagwan Vishnu, a girl appeared from Bhagwan Vishnu’s body, which was very beautiful and attractive, and she had abundance of power, with her absolute power she was carrying all divine weapons. She was part of Bhagwan’s Tej (Tapa Light)”. Moor saw this girl, and felt that she is just a girl., and that for him to remove this obstacle before he could pounce on camly sleeping Vishnu is very easy. So he entered into war of words and thought of fighting with her. The war began, Girl being an able warrior and a complete manifested energy of Baghwan Shri Vishnu Himself knew complete art of war, with her one roaring blow she grounded the Moor Dhanav on the ground and with her fearful fire of anger Moor turned into heap of ashes. When Moor died Bhagwan Vishnu woke up from His meditative sleep and saw Moor dead and asked, “this enemy of Mine, who was so horrible and furious who killed him”?

    Boon
    Girl replied “Swami! With, Your Prasad of blessing and energy, I killed him”. Bhagwan said “Kal-yani! Your this Karma (action) has made Triloks’s Munis and Devtas happy and joyous, what ever pleases you, do ask Me and I shall grant you that as a boon. Whatever you wish, although it may be not possible for Devtas to obtain, but I will surely grant you and there is no doubt in it”.

    Named Ekadashi
    This Girl was none other than Devi Ekadashi herself. She said, “Prabhu! If, You are pleased with my Karma (action) than with Your Grace I want to be Devi who is leader in Tirth (holy place), who can destroy all the obstacle, and giver of all Siddhi’s (perfection's / attainment's / accomplishment's) Riddhi's (Wealth and well-being) and Nidhi's (valuable possessions). Janar-dhan! All, persons who have Bhakthi in You and who will keep my Vrat on my Ekadashi (11 - Vedic tithi) day should get all the Siddhis. Bhagwan! Those who keep Upvas, Nakt-Vrat, and Ek-Bukt- Vrat, You (Bhagwan Vishnu) should give them Dhan (wealth), Dharma (religious righteousness), Moksha (salvation) and above all your Bakti (Shri Hari Devotion)”.

    Wish granted
    Shri Vishnu said:- "Kal-yani! What ever you have said is being fulfilled"!

    Wish granted
    Bhagwan Shri Krishna continued: - “Arjun! Receiving such blessing, Maha-vrata Ekadashi was very happy and content. Both paksh (side) Ekadashis will equally do well for human beings; therefore do not differentiate Sukla and Krishna Paksh Ekadashi. These are the days of the waxing and waning of the Moon and that they are most dear to Me. Other days i.e Trtiya (the third day), Astami (the eighth day), Navami (the ninth day), Caturdasi (the fourteenth day), are also dear to me and ofcourse especially Ekadashi (the eleventh day)” and this is beyound a doubt..

    “The merit one attains by fasting on Ekadashi is greater than that achieved by observing any other kind of fast or by going to a place of pilgrimage, and even greater than that achieved by giving charity to Brahmins I tell you most emphatically that this is true. Having thus given the Devi His benediction, Purna Prushotam suddenly disappeared. From that time onward the Ekadashi day became most meritorious and famous all over the universe. 0 Arjun, if a person strictly observes Ekadashi, I kill all his enemies and grant him the highest destination. Indeed, if a person observes this great Ekadashi fast in any of the prescribed ways, I remove all obstacles to his spiritual progress and grant him the perfection of life”.

    “Thus, 0 son of Pratha, I have described to you the origin of Ekadashi. This one day removes all sins eternally. Indeed, it is the most meritorious day for destroying all kinds of sins, and it has appeared in order to benefit everyone in the universe by bestowing all varieties of perfection”.

    “Whoever completely fasts on Ekadashi, following the rules and regulations, will achieve the supreme abode of Bhagwan Vishnu, who rides upon Garud. They are glorious who devote themselves to Bhagwan Vishnu and spend all their time studying the glories of Ekadashi, One who vows not to eat anything on Ekadashi, but to eat only on the next day achieves the same merit as one who executes a horse sacrifice. Of this there is no doubt.

    On Duvadasi, the day after Ekadashi, one should pray, Pundarikaks, Kamal-nain, now I will eat. Please shelter me."After saying this, the wise devotee should offer some flowers and water at Bhagwan’s Charan kamal and invite the Bhagwan to eat by chanting the eight-syllable mantra “Shri Krishna Sharanam Mama” thrice. If the devotee wants to gain the fruit of his fast, he should then drink water taken from the sanctified vessel in which he offered water at Charan kamal”.

    “A devotee of Bhagwan Vishnu, who day and night hears these all-auspicious Lilas concerning Bhagwan from the mouth of another devotee will be elevated to the Bhagwan Vishnu’s planet and reside there for ten million kalpas. And one who hears even one sentence about the glories of Ekadashi is freed from the reactions to such sins as killing a Brahmin. There is no doubt of this. For all eternity there will be no better way of worshiping Bhagwan Vishnu than observing a fast on Ekadashi”.
