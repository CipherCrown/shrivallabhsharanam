    'Kankarwad' a village in the 'Talang District' situated on the southern banks of 'River Krishna', in the vicinity of 'Mount Vyo-mas-thamba' in what is presently known as 'Andhra Pradesh' in 'India' there lived a 'YAJURVEDI', THIRTY THIRD in line, BHARAT-DWAJ GOTRA, one TALANG BRAHMIN named SHRI NARAYAN DIXIT also known as SHRI YAGNANARAYAN BHATT (KAMBHAMPATY). He was Shri Ved Avtaar. He was a very great scholar he had memorised all The Four Vedas and Six Shas-tras. Many rajas & rich people were his students. He being ardent devotee of Shri Baghwan Krishna, saw a auspicious moment and performed SOM YAGYA. He kept on performing SOM YAGYA'S till he reached 32nd SOM YAGYA, at that moment a voice was heard from the KUND altar “Narayan Bhatt you are very fortunate PURNAH: PUR-SHO-TAM has accepted your SOM YAGYA'S therefore PURNA PRUSOTAM SHRI KRISHNA will be born in your family when 100th SOM YAGYA will be completed within your family. At the sametime SHRI MADAN MOHANJI appeared from the altar [AGNI KUND]. This is One of the seven SAWROOPS. SHRI MADAN MOHANJI'S Haveli is in KAMVAN-VIRAJ.

    Lots of people praised SHRI NARAYAN DIXIT. He taught VED to thousand of PANDITS, but few disbelivers said what is great in teaching VED to human being , can he teach Ved to this buffalo. On hearing this he took stick and touched the buffalo and buffalo started to recited the Vedic Hymns. His son GANGADHAR BHATT or GANGADHAR DIXIT was also great scholar, he use to offer everyday food to Brahmins give Dhan (Money) to them. He performed twenty 28 SOM YAGYA'S. His fame spread and people used to call him as SHRI SHIVJI’S Avtaar. One of the pandits told him SHIV had GANGAJI IN HIS HAIR, so GANGADHARJI untied his hair the GANGAJI started flowing from his hairs. His son, GANPATI DIXIT also known as GANAPATI BHATT who performed 30 SOM YAGA'S. He taught thousands of people, so people praised him and called him as GANPATI’S Avtaar. But one of them told I will believe only if he rain rats. On hearing so he rained rats around 16 miles diameter for period 3 hours. 45 minutes. This gave him a lot of fame. His son VALLAB DIXIT also known as VALLABH BHATT who was glorious. He performed 5 SOM YAGYA'S. He was known as SURIYA NARAYAN’S Avtaar. But few of them against him said if he is SURIYA NARYAN’S Avtaar then we want to see SUN SHINE in the middle of the night. He did midday, in 12 kos area, at midnight!!!. His son LAXMAN BHATT (SHRI VASUDEV’S Avtaar) performed 5 SOMYAGYA and thus 100th yagya was performed and he heard from the sky that PURNA PURSOTAM will be born .
