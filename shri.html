    ASH-TAKSH-SHAR MAHA MANTRA

    This divine 8 letter word also known as the ASH-TAKSH-SHAR MAHA MANTRA can change our lives completely.

    Each letter is like a gift of god to us if understood letter by letter it is a simple way to all our needs and a guaranteed way to Shri Krishna’s heart.


    SHRI

    GOOD FORTUNE 'SAU-BHA-GHYA' , WEALTH 'DHAN' , FAVOURITE IN THE EYES OF THE KING 'RAJA KA PREYAH'

    This means we get all materialistic things in life by simply chanting “SHRI” the first letter of the ASH-TAKSH-SHAR MAHA MANTRA.


    KRAH

    DESTROYS ALL OUR SINS 'PAAP KA NAASH KARTA HAI'

    Clear heart gives rise to a clear soul until and unless we don’t get rid of all our sins we cannot take the entry to this devotional path 'BHAKTI MARGH' so this second letter “KRAH” gets us out of all our sins.


    SHANAH:

    DESTROYS ALL KINDS OF PAINS AND TROUBLES IN LIFE “THAT IS OF MATERIALISTIC WORLD, HEAVENLY WORLD AND SPIRITUALWORLD 'AA-DI-BAU-TIK,AA-DIYAT-MIK, AA-DHI-DE-VIK DUKHOON KA NASH KARTA HAI'

    Pure and clean heart comes only when we get away from all the pains and troubles of our life so this third letter “SHANAH:” gets us away from our pains hence purifying our soul.


    SHA

    IT RELIEVES US FROM THE CYCLE OF BIRTH AND DEATH 'JANAM MARAN KA CHAKAR NAHI HOTA HAI'

    By chanting “SHA” we liberated ourselves from the cycle of birth and death. We don’t have to enter the great cycle of 84 lakhs lives 'YONIS' thus freeing us from our self ego and pride and giving us the privilege of knowing Shri Krishna’s Anand Swarup.


    RH:

    KNOWLEDGE ABOUT BHAGWAN SHRI KRISHNA: GROWS 'PRABHU SAM-BHAN-DHI GHYAN MILTA HAI'

    Now having the knowledge of worldly fascinations being momentary our curiosity to know and how to please Shri Krishna rises and this fifth letter “RH:” gives us the privilege and knowledge to know Shri Krishna better.


    R-NAM:

    LOVE AND DEVOTION TOWARDS SHRI KRISHNA BECOMES STRONG 'PRABHU MEIN DRADH VISHWAS AUR BHAKTI MILTI HAI'

    After knowing Shri Krishna’s glory 'MAHAT-MIYA' your love and affection for Shri Kkrishna rises and this is known as 'BHAKTI' devotion. Bhakti is not easily attainable to all but this sixth letter “R-NAM:” graces us with his Bhakti.


    M:

    GIVES MORE LOVE AND AFFECTION TOWARDS OUR GURUDEV WHO PROVIDED US THE PATHWAY TO KRISHNA’S BHAKTI 'GURU MEIN VISHWAS BANTA HAI'

    To enter this path of devotion 'BHAKTI MARG' we need 'SHRI GURUDEV' a teacher, who shows us the path to meet Shri Krishna. He graces us the privilege of seva hence this seventh letter “M :” gives us the love for our guru who provided us with this divine direction and has enlightened our ignorant minds.


    M:

    UNION OF OUR SOUL WITH SHRI KRISHNA 'PRABHU KE SAATH SAYU-JYA HOTA HAI'

    Now that our souls are enlightened with the knowledge and love for Shri Krishna the last letter “M :” gives us the privilige of unifying with shri krishna. 'SAYU-JYA'


    Every letter of SHRI ASH-TAKSH-SHAR has divine capability to grant its devotees all their wishes and fulfil their desires. Hence devote some time of your daily life to chant this ASH-TAKSH-SHAR MAHA MANTRA and enrich your entire personality and soul.
