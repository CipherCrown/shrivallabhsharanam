    ||||CHIN-TA KA-PI NA KAR-YAH: NI-VEDI-TAT-MA-BHIHI KA-DA-PEE-TI||
    ||BHAGAVAN-API PUSHTI-STHO NA KARI-SHYA-TI LAU-KI-KEEM CHA GA-TIM||||............1

    He who has offered himself to God should never have worries of any kind. The Gracious Bhagwan will not confine you to terrsetrial fate.

    ||||NI-VE-DA-NAM TU SMAR-TAV-YAM SAR-VAT-HA TAD-RUSHAIR-JAN-AIHI||
    ||SARVESH-VARA-SCHA SAR-VAT-MA NI-JE-CHHAT-AHA KARISH-YATI||||............2

    We should always remember the text of the ceremonious offering of ourselves to God, in the company of His devotees. The Omnipotent (all powerful) and Omniscient (knowing everything) God will perform as He wishes.

    ||||SAR-VE-SHAM PRA-BHU-SAM-BHAND-HO NA PRA-TEK-MITI-STHI-TIHI||
    ||ATO-NYA-VINI-YO-GEPI CHIN-TA KA SVA-SYA SOPI-CHET||||............3

    Everyone (including your close relatives) bears relationship with God. Worry not if any one of them is not so well devoted to God as you expect. Because, through your surrender to God's will, they too will freely surrender to His will.

    ||||AG-YA-NAD-THAVA GYA-NAT KRUT-MAT-MA-NI-VE-DA-NAM
    ||YEH: KRISHNA-SAT KRUT-PRA-NAI-STESH-AM KA PARI-DE-VNA||||............4

    He who has knowingly or unknowingly, offered himself to God and united his soul with Bhagwan Krishna, must not worry.

    ||||TATHA NI-VE-DANE CHIN-TA TYA-JYA SHREE-PURU-SHOT-TAME||
    ||VI-NI-YO-GEPI SA TYA-JYA SAMAR-THO HI HARI-HI-SVATH:||||............5

    Likewise shun any anxiety about the Bhagwan acceptance of your submission to Him. The most Gracious and All Powerful God will be your saviour, even if your submission to Him is imperfect.

    ||||LOKE SWAS-THYAM TAT-HA VE-DE HARIS-TYU NA KARISH-YATI||
    ||PUSH-TI-MARG-STITOH YAS-MA-TA-SAK-SHI-NO BHA-VTA-KHI-LAHA||||............6

    In terrestrial and scriptural matters the Bhagwan will not let you have any ease, because He is most Gracious (and will give you comfort in spiritual matters). So, on this subject, all of you should be mere spectators (and submit to the will of God).

    ||||SEVA-KRU-TI-R-GURO-RA-GYA BA-DHA-NAM VA HARI-ICH-CHA||
    ||ATA-HA SEVA-PARAM CHIT-TAM VI-DHA-YA STHI-YA-TAM SUK-HAM||||............7

    Worship the Bhagwan as per your Guru's directions. If, however, (through divine inspiration) you experience that His will is different (from directions of your Guru) you should act accordingly (if for Bhagwan's comfort and not yours) in which ease, worry not about having deviated from Guru's directions. Engross your mind in Bhagwan's worship and live cheerfully.

    ||||CHIT-TOD-VE-GAM VI-DHA-YA-PI HARI-YAD-YAT-KARI-SHYA-TI||
    ||TA-THAI-VA TAS-YA LEE-LETI MAT-VA CHIN-TAM DRU-TAM TYA-JET||||............8

    Even when your mind feels distressed, feel assured that it is God's divine activity (ultimately for your benfit) and so, promptly rid yourself of worries.

    ||||TAS-MAT-SAR-VAT-MANA NIT-YAM "SHRI-KRISHNA-HA-SHA-RA-NAM-MAMA"||
    ||VA-DAD-BHI-REV SA-TA-TAM STHE-YA-MI-TYE-VA ME MA-TIHI||||............9

    Still (if you cannot get rid of worries), recite, at all times, with complete devotion the Mantra
    "Shri Krishna Sharanam Mama". Such is the perscription of Shri Vallabh (for ridding your worries).

    || ITTI SHRIVALLABHASACHARYAVEERACHITAM NAVRATNAM SAMPURNAM ||
