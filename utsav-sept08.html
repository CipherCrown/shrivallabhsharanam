    UTSAVS
    FOR THE MONTH OF
    September - 2008

    Tuesday 2nd September, 2008 - (3rd Bhadhra Sukla Paksh),
    Samvadini” shravani

    Wednesday 3rd September, 2008 - (4th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh),
    Ganesh Chatruthi
    (Birth of Shri Ganesh.)

    Thursday 4th September, 2008 - (5th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh ),
    Rushi panchami & Shri Chandravali’s Utsav
    (Birthday of Shri Chandravaliji.)

    Friday 5th September, 2008 - (6th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh),
    Tilkayat Shri Vithaleshji’s Utsav v.s. 1744. & Shri Lalithaji’s Utsav.
    (Birthday of Shri Vithaleshji’s and Shri Lalithaji’s.)

    Monday 8th September, 2008 - (8th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh),
    Radha-astami
    (Birthday of Shri Radhaji.)

    Thursday 11th September, 2008 - (11th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh ),
    Dhan Ekhadashi, nine lac. granth-karta (creation of books) Shri Purshotamji’s Utsav v.s. 1714.
    (Ekadsahi fast and Birthday of Shri Purshotamji.)

    Friday 12th September, 2008 - (12th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh ),
    Vaman-duadashi
    (Shri Vamanji’s birthday.)

    Monday 15th September, 2008 - (15th Bhadhra Sukla Paksh ),
    Start of Sanji.
    (Decorating front door of temple with different colours of flower petals, coloured grated coconut etc.)

    Tuesday 16th September, 2008 - (1st Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Start of Sharadh
    (Offering of oblation and food to the death ones according to Tithi (vedic date of death person). )

    Friday 19th September, 2008 - (4th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Shri Hariraiji’s utsav v.s. 1647
    (Birthday of Shri Hariraiji.)

    Monday 22nd September, 2008 - (8th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Shri Gopinathji’s son Shri Pursotamji’s utsav v.s.1587
    (Birthday of Shri Pursotamji.)

    Thursday 25th September, 2008 - (11th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Indra Ekadashi Vrat
    (Ekadashi Fast.)

    Friday 26th September, 2008 - (12th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Shri Mahaprabhuji’s elder son Shri Gopinathji’s utsav v.s. 1567
    (Birthday of Shri Gopinathji’s.)

    Saturday 27th September, 2008 - (13th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Shri Gusaiji’s third son Shri Balkrishnaji’s Utsav v.s. 1606
    (Birthday of Shri Balkrishnaji.)

    Monday 29th September, 2008 - (30th Aswin Krishna Paksh),
    Sarva-pitru Amavas and end of Sanji
    (If you miss any ones Tithi (date) of death of loved and dear ones you can perform on this day.)

    Tuesday 30th September, 2008 - (1st Aswin sukla Paksh),
    Matama Shrad & Navratri
    (Beginning of Navratri.)
