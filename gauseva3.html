    GAU-SEVA

    The easy and best way of purifying our earning is to do Gau-seva (serve Cows), but how?

    Do Gau’seva in our daily routine of life. It is neither time consuming nor it takes much effort from our end? “Quick and effective” is the slogan of todays fast pace life. Just go back in our childhood when our parents bought us a moneybox and taught us how to save. Similarly, we being like children have to have a moneybox to train ourselves to save money for Gau-seva.

    What should we do? How can we perform Gau-seva?

    Physically of course, but how many of us have the time? Even if, some people have time then are we staying in close proximity of any Gau-Shala, in most cases the answer will be “No”. Then we are left with just one choice to help all those people who are already doing Gau-seva,but how to help them and what type of help can we do?

    Financial help is only last resort for us. We can do this by cultivating a habit to drop some percentage from our monthly earning in the moneybox named as ”Gau-Seva box”, according to our capacity. This procedure will certainly help us to collect a substantially good amount in a year for Gau-seva and this will also result in purifying our earnings, thus removing all obstacles from our life and blessing us with vision of rightness. Through this seva you have unknowing served 330 million Devtas, which dwell in cow’s body, proof of this can be found in Vedas.

    Children can help in doing this Gau-seva (serve Cows)

    Train your children to save some money from their pocket money from childhood! We should teach them to drop a coin in Gau-Seva box everyday from their pocket money as we did in our childhoods.

    This habit of dropping money will not only make them clever, wise and religious but also their mind will never go on the wrong path of life because 330 million Devtas’s blessing are always with them. Serving cows, who are the dearest to Purna Purshotam Bhagwan Shri Krishna, means you have served Purna Purshotam Bhagwan Shri Krishna, Himself.

    Where to send Gua-seva money we have collected

    Normally, it is advisable to ask this question from your Guru, Vallabh-kul-balak who has blessed you with Brama-samband. In case, you have larger amount then you should take advice from your Guru about the Shri Vallabh’s Bhatakji, where there is no proper housing for cows.

    It is my personal request to all Vaishnavas to see that every Bhatakji has proper Gau-shala with at least one to two cows. If you cannot be in touch with your Guru or any Vallabh-kul-balak, do contact me. It will be my pleasure to advice you about some Gau-shala locations, where your seva reaches the right place and for the right purpose. You can contact me, i.e., Navin Ramdas Bhatia: navin@shrivallabhsharanam.com.
