    ||| SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - Glory of the Gita |||


    || SHRI GANESHAYA NAMAH! || || SHRI GOPALAKRISHNAYA NAMAH! ||

    Prostrations to Shi Ganesha ! Prostrations to ShriKrishna, thelover of Radha !


    |||| DHAROVAACHA: ||||
    |||| BHAGWAN PARAMESAANA BHAKTIRAVYABHICHAARINEE; ||
    || PRARABDHAM BHUJYAMAANASYA KATHAM BHAVATI HE PRABHO. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 1 SHLOKA}

    The earth said:
    O Bhagwan, the supreme Lord ! How can unflinching devotion arise in him who is immersed in his Prarabdha Karmas (worldly life), O Bhagwan?


    |||| SHRI VISHNURUVAACHA: ||||
    |||| PRAARABDHAM BHUJYAMAANO HI GEETAABHYAASARATAH SADAA; ||
    || SA MUKTAH SA SUKHEE LOKE KARMANAA NOPALIPYATE. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 2 SHLOKA}

    Lord Vishnu said:
    Though engaged in the performance of worldly duties, one who is regular in the study of the Gita, becomes free. He is the happy man in this world. He is not bound by Karma.


    |||| MAHAAPAAPAADIPAAPAANI GEETADHYAANAM KAROTI CHET; ||
    || KWACHIT SPARSAM NA KURVANI NALINEEDALAM AMBUVAT. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 3 SHLOKA}

    Just as the water stains not the lotus leaf, even so, sins do not taint him who is regular in the recitation of the Gita.


    |||| GEETAYAAH PUSTAKAM YATRA YATRA PAARHAH PRAVARTATE; ||
    || TATRA SARVAANI TEERTHANI PRAYAAGAADEENI TATRA VAI. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 4 SHLOKA}

    All the sacred places of pilgrimage, like Prayag, etc., dwell in that place where the book, the Gita is kept, and where the Gita is read.


    |||| SARVE DEVAASCHA RISHAYO YOGINAH PANNAGAASCHA YE; ||
    || GOPAALAA GOPIKAA VAAPI NAARADODDHAVAPAARSHADAIH. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 5 SHLOKA}

    All the gods, sages, Yogins, divine serpents, Gopalas, Gopikas (friends and devotees of Bhagwan Krishna), Narada, Uddhava and others (dwell here).


    |||| SAHAAYO JAAYATE SEEGHRAM YATRA GEETAA PRAVARTATE; ||
    || YATRA GEETAAVICHAARASCHA PATHANAM PAATHANAM SRUTAN; |||
    ||| TATRAAHAM NISCHITAM PRITHVI NIVASAAMI SADAIVA HI. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 6 SHLOKA}

    Help comes quickly where the Gita is recited and, O Earth, I dwell at times where the Gita is read, heard, taught and contemplated upon.


    |||| GEETAASRAYE 'HAM RISHTHAAMI GEETAA ME CHOTTAMAM GRIHAM; ||
    || GEETAAJNAANAM UPAASRITYA TREEN LOKAAN PAALAYAAMYAHAM. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 7 SHLOKA}

    I take refuge in the Gita, and the Gita is MY best abode. I protect the three worlds with the knowledge of the Gita.


    |||| GEETAA ME PARAMAA VIDYAA BRAHMAROOPAA NA SAMSAYAH; ||
    || ARDHAMAATRAAKSHARAA NITYAA SWAANIRVAACHYAPADAATMIKAA. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 8 SHLOKA}

    The Gita is My highest science, which is doubtless of the form of Brahman, the Eternal, the Ardhamatra (of the Pranava Om), the ineffable splendour of the Self.


    |||| CHIDAANANDENA KRISHNENA PROKTAA SWAMUKHATO 'RJUNA; ||
    || VEDATRAYEE PARAANANDAA TAWAARTHAJNAANASAMYUTAA. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 9 SHLOKA}

    It was spoken by the blessed Krishna, the all-knowing, through His own mouth, to Arjuna. It contains the essence of the three Vedas, the knowledge of the Reality. It is full of supreme bliss.


    |||| YOASHTAADASA JAPO NITYAM NARO NISCHALAMAANASAH; ||
    || JNAANASIDDHIM SA LABHATE TATO YAATI PARAM PAADM. ||||
    {SHRIMAD BHAGWAD GITA MAHATMYA - 10 SHLOKA}

    He who recites the eighteen chapters of the Gita daily, with pure, unshaken mind, attains perfection in knowledge, and reaches the highest state or supreme goal.
