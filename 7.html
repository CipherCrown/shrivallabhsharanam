    Understanding the Alokik (divine) power of "Shri Aacha-riya-charan Shri Madh-Vallabh-acha-riya-ji", the GURU, of Pustimargiya Vaishnav's.


    You established six Lilas on the Earth:

    • Your Aavtaar on the earth is to pardon Devi Jeva's (Divine Souls) of its sins, to establish (jeva's) relation wth Bhagwan and to enjoy the Lila Darshan.
    • Based on Shri-Madh-Bhagwat-ji You showed the way in Subodh-ni-ji's, Tika (an extended and descriptively elaborate work on Shri Madh-Baghwad).
    • You defeated Maya-vaad (Material Illusion) and established Nishkam Bhakti Margh (Path of Selfless Devotion).
    • Three times You had Prithvi Pradh-shina and by this way You accepted Satvik, Rajasik and Tamasik Bhakth.
    • Through Pusti Margh You showed the strength Lovable Seva and accepted Stree (Female) and Shudra's.
    • Aa-achariya's, Sevak's, and learned people of those time were shown miracles and made them believe in Pusti Marg.

    Highlights of some
    of Important events
    of Shri Vallabh's life.
    Year wise Divine Profile:

    • ||1535 || Pra-gha-tiya Ustav (Time of Avtaar) where You showed:--

      • Your Agni Swaroop.

      • You are Shri Krishna himself by creating Ratna Jarit Mahal (Nandalay-Palace made of Precious Stones) where in Jan-mash-tami Maha Ustav was celebrated with all the Gop's and Gopi's, Nand-baba & Jaso-da Maiya herself along with Vrash-bhan-ji & Kirti-ji.


    • ||1545 || Sash-trat in Jagdish-raj's sabha where in You were the winner.



    • ||1546 || Sash-trat with Ghat Sarsvati in Ujjain in Viduth's sabha and you defeated him and freed Sarsvati-ji.



    • ||1548 || Vidya-nagar Viduth's sabha where in you had Sash-trat with opponents and was victorious.



    • ||1549 || Elaborate Sash-trat in Kashi with Dundhi-raj Yogi.



    • ||1550 || At midnight on Thakurani Ghat Shri Goverdhan-dharan Shri-Nathji appeared and explained to Him about Brahma-samband Diksha.



    • ||1553 || Shri Vallabh performed first Prithivi Parikrama.



    • ||1556 || At Mount Shri Goverdhan He did Stha-pana of Shri-Nathji's Swaroop, and established Shri-Nathji's Seva.



    • ||1558 || Shri Vallabh performed second Prithivi Parikrama.



    • ||1558 || Shri Vallabh got married with Divine Shri Maha-Laxmi-Ji, the daughter of Shri Madumangal



    • ||1558 || In Kashi Sash-trat through Patra-valam-ban and was victorious.



    • ||1565 || Sash-trat in Vidya-nagar in the sabha of Krishna-dev Raja and was honored with a letter declaring Him as victorious. At the same time the king honored Him with Kanka-abhishek (a bath with gold coins). Shri Vallabh did not accept those coins & told king to distribute it to Brahmins.



    • ||1566 || Shri Vallabh performed third Prithivi Parikrama.



    • ||1566 || Shri Vallabh took Shri Maha-Laxmi-ji to Adel, Your home town.



    • ||1567 || In Ashwin Krishna 12 ,Your first son, Shri Gopi-nath-ji was born.



    • ||1572 || In Posh Krishna 9, Your second son Shri Vithal-nath-ji was born.



    • ||1587 || You had taken a vow of total Silence and Brahma-chari for Fourty Days.



    • ||1587 || In Aashad Shukla 3 at mid-day you entered Shri Ganga-ji and disappeared with a ball of fire which arose from Gangaji when You entered in it. This ball of fire disappeared in the sky.

    Grantha's (Books) written by Him:

    You have written lots of Granthas in shot a Sagar (ocean), but most important are 84, few of them are Sodas-Granth (Sixteen Books), Shri Subodh-niji (based on Shri Madh Bhagwat-ji).>

    His Sevaks:

    You have un-accountable Sevaks it includes everybody living and non-living beings, but the most important are 84 Vaishnavas, they in other words are the pillars of Pusti Margh.

    Prithivi Parikramas:

    During Your three Prithivi Parikramas You traveled across the entire Bharat Varsh, but 84 places are most important where You recited Shri-Madh-Bhagwat. These places are known as 84 Bathakji's.

    Your Pratis-tha-pit and Seviya Swaroops:

    There are more than 51 different Swaroops:

    Shri Bal-Krishna-lal {20}
    Shri Madan Mohan-ji {10}
    Shri Navneet-priya-ji {4}
    Shri Mathuresh-ji {3}
    Shri Vithal-nath-ji
    Shri Dwarka-nath-ji
    Shri Gokal-nath-ji
    Shri Chandra-ma-ji
    Shri Aasht-bhu-ja-ji
    Shri Dhamodhar-ji
    Shri Kaliyan-rai-ji
    Shri Nathji {4}
    Shri Lalit-tribhung-ji
    Shri Ladlesh-ji
    Shri Mukund-rai-ji.
