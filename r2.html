    Q: What is Shri Vallab's mother's name?

    A: His mother's name is Shri ILL-LAMA-GARU-JI.

    Q: Was she also from the same place?

    A: No. She was from Vidyanagar.

    Q: Can you tell the whereabouts of the ancestors of Mahaprabhu Vallabhacharya?

    A: In 14th Century, on the bank of Krishna River there was a village named Kakarvada. There resided a genius sage named Yagya Narayan Bhatt, who used to perform YAJNAS (Sacrifices) worshipping God with Holy Fire in altar daily.

    Q: Did he gain anything from it?

    A: Once Vishnu Muni came there, when he was performing the YAJNA

    Q: Did he bless him?

    A: He bestowed upon Yagya Narayan Bhatt 'Gopal Mantra' with the help of which desires can be fulfilled. Sri Yagya Narayan was much pleased, and upto his last breath he did SOM YAGNAS.

    Q: Was Vallabhacharya the fruit of that Yajna?

    A: Yes! When Yagya Narayan was on the last stage of his life, God Vishnu appeared before him in a vision and told him that after the completion of 100 Som Yajnas in your family, I myself will take birth (incarnate) in your Home.

    Q: When were the 100 Som Yajnas over?

    A: After the completion of 32 Som Yajna's Shri Yagya Narayan Bhatt departed to Nitya Lila, his son Gangadhar Bhatt carried on this Holy performance alongwith Holy writing. He Completed 28 Som Yajna's.

    Q: When did he complete hundred Yajnas?

    A: Gangadhar Bhatt's Son Ganpati Bhatt completed 30 Som Yajnas and 10 still remaining to be performed in the chain of 100. In this way ninety Som Yajnas were completed during three generations.

    Q: Who completed remaining ten Yajnas?

    A: Vallabh Bhatt, son of Gangadhar Bhatt completed Five Som Yajnas, and wrote ten granthas, scripts on Devotion (Bhakti).

    Q: It means only ninety-five; Som Yajnas were over till Vallabh Bhatt's time, the fourth generation?

    A: Vallabh Bhatt, who was also called Balam Bhatt, had two sons, Laxman Bhatt and Janardan Bhatt. Laxman Bhatt got married with two daughters of Royal religious priest (Raj Purohit) Susharma, of Vidyanagar.

    Q: What were their names?

    A: Shri Yella-ma-garu and Shri ILL-LAMA-GARU.

    Q: Was Laxman Bhatt's one wife living with him and another with her father?

    A: No! after the destruction of Kakarvada Laxman Bhatt left for Agrahar Nagar along with both wives, where the son Rama Krishna and daughters Subhadra and Saraswati were born. At this place doing 5 Yajnas, he completed 100 Som Yajnas, a pre-condition to Purna-Purshotam's Avtar.

    Q: Any distinguished matter took place after 100 Som Yajnas?

    A: He was assured by angel (Dev-doot); 'Purna Purushottam' will take birth in your family. In response to fulfillment of hundred Yajnas Laxman Bhatt performed a feast to hundred Brahmins in Varanasi.

    Q: Did Laxman Bhatt settle his family in Varanasi?

    A: Yes, but after a few days owing to Muslim invaders on Kashi he had to leave with his family for South, again.

    Q: Wasn't any brother to Mahaprabhu's father?

    A: Yes.

    Q: What was his name?

    A: Shri Janardan Bhatt.

    Q: Where was the Divine Baby born? What was his condition at birth?

    A: Divine baby was born in Champaranya, his delivery was pre-mature. (Remember all Godly acts are Divine in nature, they are Alo-kik, not wordly).
